Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21

This policy explains what the mobile game Bubble Pop (package com.pilogame.bubblepop, “the App”, “we”), published by Pilogame, collects, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • The App is a single-player arcade game. You do not need an account and we do not run our own servers for it.
  • Your game progress (stages, stars, gems, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • The App currently shows no advertising.
  • We never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location permissions.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (unlocked stages, stars, score, gems, achievements), settings (sound, vibration, language, touch-button layout) are saved in the App’s private storage on your device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes them.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to measure how the game is used and to improve it. The App sends events such as: app opened, screens viewed (home, journey, shop…), stage started and finished (stage number, stars, time), gems earned and spent, achievements unlocked, and purchase button taps.

Firebase also collects automatically: an app-instance identifier, the device’s advertising ID where available, device model, operating system version, app version, language and approximate location derived from the IP address (country/region). This data is not used to identify you personally.

Analytics storage is on. Because the App shows no ads, advertising-related signals (ad storage, ad user data, ad personalization) are always sent as “denied”.

Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses data from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

The shop sells packs of gems (in-game currency). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play under Google’s terms; we do not receive your card or billing details. The App receives a purchase token and the product ID from Google Play to deliver your gems and to restore purchases.

Advertising

The App currently contains no advertising SDK and shows no ads. If ads are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released and you will be asked for consent where required.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state — to send analytics and to contact Google Play for purchases.
  • Vibration — for haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings).
  • Advertising ID — read by Google Analytics for Firebase as described above. You can reset or delete it in your device’s Google settings.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and deletion

Data on your device stays until you clear the App’s data or uninstall it. Analytics data is kept by Google Analytics for up to 14 months and is then deleted automatically. You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings at any time. To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your device, contact us (see below).

Security

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We keep the information we have to the minimum needed to run and improve the game.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be noted in the App’s release notes.

Contact
